About this space
Welcome to The Prairie Ranch House, a cozy stay in the heart of Champaign.

Location:
Just minutes from the University of Illinois, Memorial Stadium, State Farm Center, Art Mart, Jarling’s Custard Cup, and favorite local cafés, this charming ranch-style home offers relaxing, comfortable spaces perfect for studying, working remotely, or spending time with friends and family.

Located in a quiet, friendly neighborhood, you’ll enjoy peace and privacy while staying close to everything Champaign has to offer.


The space: 
This fully renovated single-family home features 3 comfortable bedrooms (2 queen beds and 1 full bed) and 1 modern bathroom, making it an ideal stay for families, friends, or visiting groups. You’ll enjoy a spacious, open layout with cozy living areas and a fully fenced backyard — perfect for relaxing outdoors or letting children play safely.


Parking: 
Parking is easy and convenient, with a private 1-car garage and a tree-shaded driveway that offers both comfort and peace of mind throughout your stay.


Kitchen:
The kitchen is fully stocked and ready for your stay, featuring a stove, oven, microwave, coffee maker, blender, air fryer, toaster, and a refrigerator with a newly replaced filter and built-in ice maker — perfect for everything from quick breakfasts to full home-cooked meals.

Other things to note: 
We are happy to welcome dogs with prior approval. Cats and other pets are not allowed.
* Maximum of 2 dogs, unless approved in advance.
* Please let us know the dog’s breed, size before booking.
* Dogs must be house-trained and well-behaved.
* Dogs are not allowed on beds, sofas, or other furniture.
* Dogs should not be left alone in the house unless they are safely crated.
* Please clean up all dog waste from the backyard before checkout.
* Please wipe dogs’ paws before entering the house on rainy or muddy days.

The shower diverter is built into the tub spout. To use the shower, just turn on the water and pull down on the end of the spout — this will redirect the water to the showerhead.

The dryer is in the laundry area near the backyard door. Open the white sliding door, and you’ll see it right there.

The high chair and crib are also stored in the laundry area. You’ll find them near the white sliding door.
